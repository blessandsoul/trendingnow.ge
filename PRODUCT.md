# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Georgian shoppers and search visitors who want to discover products that are gaining attention, compare a clear starting price, and decide whether to add an item to a local order list.

## Product Purpose

TrendingNow is a Georgian marketplace storefront for product discovery. The primary job is to make the next useful product easy to find, inspect, save, and add to a local cart. The catalog is source-backed. Final price, availability, and delivery require confirmation before an order.

## Brand Personality

Decisive, practical, locally fluent. It should feel like a confident shopping destination, not a copied marketplace shell, a news dashboard, or an AI experiment.

## Anti-references

- Copied Wildberries or Ozon visual identity, names, assets, or copy.
- Giant promotional banners that hide the product catalog.
- Warm paper boutique styling, generic purple SaaS effects, and dense terminal dashboards.
- Invented stock, delivery promises, reviews, or trend metrics.

## Design Principles

- Put discovery and search before marketing language.
- Let real product imagery, price context, and clear actions do the work.
- Use a single signal color only for priority, selected state, and price movement.
- Keep dense shopping information calm, legible, and easy to scan.
- Treat every product as a decision surface, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, clear Georgian labels, visible keyboard focus, reduced motion support, and no information conveyed by color alone.
